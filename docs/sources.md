# Sources and attribution

Naut information and images are extracted from the user-supplied SingleFile
snapshots of these Awesomenauts Wiki articles, saved 10 October 2026:

- https://awesomenauts.fandom.com/wiki/Admiral_Swiggins
- https://awesomenauts.fandom.com/wiki/Ayla
- https://awesomenauts.fandom.com/wiki/Chucho_Krokk

Each dataset records the canonical URL, original filename, size and SHA-256.
No raw Wiki snapshots are published by the build. Quotes/theme-song lyrics
are excluded. Item icons link to their individual source articles; costs and
effects are unavailable in these snapshots.

Six map dossiers and the holiday archive derive from supplied snapshots of
Maps, Ribbit IV, Starstorm Station, AI Station 205, AI Station 404, Sorona,
Aiguillon and Holiday Themes. URLs and credits are retained in maps-data.js
and the rendered UI. Some holiday screenshots are credited by the source to
Hmmt and IRCannonFodder.

## Radio and player activity

Radio uses the official embedded player for SonicPicnic’s
[Awesomenauts — Ultimate Edition](https://sonicpicnic.bandcamp.com/album/awesomenauts-ultimate-edition),
album ID 3940808224 as supplied by Bandcamp’s album metadata. Audio is streamed
by Bandcamp, not downloaded or redistributed in this repository.

Player snapshots query Valve’s public GetNumberOfCurrentPlayers endpoint for
appid 204300. SteamDB and SteamCharts remain external reference links; no chart
pages are scraped. The fetch time is shown and unavailable data is never zero.

Character theme IDs and URLs are stored in data/ost.json, extracted from the
official Ultimate Edition album metadata on 10 October 2026. Aliases cover
Sheriff/Sherriff, Skølldir/Skolldir, Genji, Vinnie & Spike, Professor Milton
Yoolip and Qi’Tara. Each dossier opens its own official embedded track player.
Recorded Steam summaries use only our timestamped samples; they do not claim
a complete SteamCharts history or an all-time player peak.
