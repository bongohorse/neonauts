ROOKIE BRIEFING

Awesomenauts combines a 2D platformer with team strategy. This briefing covers the standard battle mode: two teams, three Nauts each, one enemy base to destroy.

⌖

THE MISSION

Destroy the enemy Solar Drill while protecting your own. Defeating enemy Nauts creates an opening; taking down the base wins the match.

01

KNOW YOUR NAUT

Learn your attacks, abilities and movement before chasing opponents. Keep a way to escape when committing to a fight.

Explore the Naut selector →
02

FOLLOW THE DROIDS

Allied droids advance towards enemy turrets. Let them take turret fire while you attack from a safer position. Clear enemy droids to protect your side.

03

COLLECT & SPEND SOLAR

Pick up Solar and earn more by defeating enemies. Spend it at the shop on upgrades that suit the match. Solar is currency, not your health.

04

FIGHT AS A TEAM

Watch the minimap. Help nearby allies and avoid fighting outnumbered. A safe retreat is often better than trading a life for damage.

05

KEEP AN EXIT

Check your health and cooldowns before going in. Pull back to recover, then return with your team instead of repeatedly rushing in alone.

06

TURN AN OPENING INTO A PUSH

When enemies retreat or fall, escort droids and attack exposed structures. Stay aware of returning defenders.

Decode the terminology →

BEFORE YOUR NEXT MATCH

  • Know which ability gets you into a fight and which gets you out.
  • Watch allied droids before approaching a turret.
  • Check the minimap before chasing an enemy.
  • Spend Solar with a purpose.

General basics, checked 10 October 2026. Exact values and available modes can differ by game version. Sources: Steam · Community beginner guide (2017) · Structures · Droids. Tactical tips are general guidance, not a prescribed build.

THE GLOSSARY

Quick definitions for new mercenaries. Community shorthand can vary; these are common meanings.

27 terms

Ability
A Naut’s special action, such as a movement skill, attack or support effect.
Basic attack / AA
The regular attack. AA commonly means auto attack in community discussions.
Build
The upgrades selected for a Naut during a match.
Burst
A large amount of damage delivered in a short time.
Cooldown / CD
The wait before an ability can be used again.
Creeps
Neutral creatures found in parts of the map, separate from lane droids.
Crowd control / CC
Effects that restrict an opponent’s movement or actions, such as slows and stuns.
DPS
Damage per second: a way to describe sustained damage.
Droids
Computer-controlled units that advance along lanes and attack enemy structures.
Farm
Collect resources by clearing enemies such as droids.
Gank
A surprise attack, often with help from another teammate.
Harass / poke
Deal damage from a relatively safe position to pressure an opponent.
Lane
A main route where droids advance and turrets defend.
Minimap
The small map overview used to track battlefield activity.
MOBA
Multiplayer online battle arena: a team game centred on characters and objectives.
Naut
A playable Awesomenauts character.
Peel
Help an ally escape by disrupting the enemies attacking them.
Push
Advance with your team or droids to pressure enemy structures.
Rotate
Move to another lane or area to help with a fight or objective.
Skin
An alternate cosmetic appearance for a Naut.
Solar
The in-match currency used to buy upgrades.
Solar Drill / base
The structure each team protects. Destroy the enemy drill to win a standard match.
Squishy
Community shorthand for a character who is relatively easy to defeat when caught.
Stun
An effect that briefly prevents the target from acting.
Tank
A durable character or a character taking damage for teammates.
Turret
A defensive structure guarding the approach to a team’s base.
Upgrade
A purchase that changes or improves a Naut’s abilities or attributes.

Game basics: Community beginner guide. General tactical vocabulary is explained in plain English; it is not an official list of Naut roles.
