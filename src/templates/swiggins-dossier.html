CREW BRIEFING

Swiggins excels with backup. Check enemy crowd control before hooking in, then coordinate a tether with nearby allies.

TACTICS: PLAY AS / WITH / AGAINST

Playing as Swiggins

Anchor Hook commits you to close range. Check your team's position before diving. Recover Homboldt after dropping it to regain Anchor Swing and access to Anchor Hook.

Playing with Swiggins

Stay nearby and follow up when he catches an enemy. Protect the anchor when it is safe to help him retrieve it.

Playing against Swiggins

Destroying Homboldt can free a tethered target. Traps and crowd control can punish his approach; without his anchor, his damage potential drops.

ABILITIES & MOVEMENT

Homboldt changes how Swiggins fights. Carry the anchor for melee attacks and Anchor Hook; drop it to tether an enemy and switch to Ink Spray.

FROM TITAN TO THE STARS

Charles Swiggins grew up on Titan as the youngest of thirty siblings in a naval family. After graduating from Wet Point, he took command of the Royal Fleet's Sweet Homboldt.

His encounter with Captain Inkbeard's Colossus left both ships wrecked. In the final fight, Swiggins seized his ship's anchor and used it to defeat the pirate captain.

Now an admiral, he carries Homboldt into space as a member of the Awesomenauts.

A NAUT MADE BY THE COMMUNITY

Swiggins originated in the 2012 Design-Your-Naut contest. Channfree created the winning concept; Coffeybean4 won the subsequent visual design contest.
